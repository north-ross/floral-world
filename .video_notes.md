# Facts
- Hawaii has more introduced plant species than native ones
- Sri Lanka has the most recorded extirpated species of any island
- Japan is ranked first in the world for moonwort fern diversity
- South Africa has the most endemic sedges
- Most saxifrage species prefer alpine climates, and their diversity is centered on the Himalayas and Sichuan
- 55 plant families are most diverse in south-central China (Yunnan/Sichuan/Chongqing), including big ones like grasses, pines, rushes, broomrape, poppy, primulaceae
- Texas has the most cactus species in the US, but Arizona has more endemics
- Asteraceae has more species, but Poaceae has a wider distribution
